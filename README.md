# Business Insights Report (EDA)

## Project Overview

This project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a marketing campaign dataset to uncover customer purchasing behavior, campaign performance, and business insights. The analysis helps identify high-performing marketing channels and provides ROI-based recommendations for better business decision-making.

---

## Objective

* Clean and analyze the marketing campaign dataset
* Visualize customer behavior and purchase patterns
* Evaluate campaign performance
* Identify high ROI marketing channels
* Generate business recommendations based on insights

---

## Tools & Technologies Used

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Jupyter Notebook

---

## Dataset Features

The dataset includes:

* Customer demographics
* Income information
* Product spending details
* Marketing campaign responses
* Purchase channels (Store, Web, Catalog)

---

## Data Cleaning Process

* Handled missing values
* Removed duplicate records
* Fixed dataset formatting issues
* Created a new feature:

  * `Total_Spending`

---

## Exploratory Data Analysis Performed

### Visualizations Created

* Income vs Total Spending Scatter Plot
* Marketing Channel Performance Bar Chart
* Campaign Acceptance Analysis
* Correlation Heatmap
* Customer Distribution Analysis

---

## Key Insights

### 1. Income vs Spending

Customers with higher income tend to spend more overall, showing a positive relationship between income and total spending.

### 2. Best Performing Marketing Channel

Store purchases generated the highest sales, followed by web purchases.

### 3. Poor Performing Channel

Catalog purchases showed the lowest customer engagement and purchase volume.

### 4. Campaign Performance

Campaigns 4 and 5 achieved the highest customer acceptance rates, while Campaign 2 performed poorly.

### 5. Cross-Selling Opportunities

Customers purchasing one product category often purchased products from other categories as well.

---

## ROI Recommendations

### Increase Budget For:

* Store Marketing
* Digital/Web Marketing
* High-performing campaigns (Campaign 4 & 5)

### Reduce Budget For:

* Catalog Marketing
* Low-performing campaigns

### Additional Recommendations

* Target high-income customers with premium offers
* Improve online conversion strategies
* Optimize weaker marketing campaigns

---

## Conclusion

The EDA process helped uncover valuable business insights regarding customer behavior, marketing effectiveness, and campaign performance. The analysis suggests that focusing on store and digital marketing channels can improve customer engagement and maximize ROI.
